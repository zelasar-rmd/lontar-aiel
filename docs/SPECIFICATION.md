## 1. Inference Energy Modeling

Energy consumption in LLM inference depends heavily on active parameter size, architectural density, and hardware accelerator efficiency (NVIDIA H100/A100, Google TPU v4/v5e).

### Baseline Energy Coefficients
* **Flash / Lightweight Distilled Models:** `0.20 Wh per 1,000 tokens`
  * *Targets:* Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude 3.5 Haiku, GPT-4o-mini, Mistral 7B.
* **Frontier / Dense Models:** `1.50 Wh per 1,000 tokens`
  * *Targets:* Gemini Pro, Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet, GPT-4o.
* **Reasoning / Heavy Models:** `3.00 Wh per 1,000 tokens`
  * *Targets:* Claude 3 Opus, OpenAI o1/o3 reasoning chains.

$$\text{Energy (Wh)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Tokens}}{1000} \right) \times \text{Model Coefficient (Wh/k-token)}$$

## 2. Carbon Footprint ($CO_2e$)

Carbon intensity varies by geographical grid and cloud provider Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE):
* **Global Cloud Datacenter Average Intensity:** `400 g CO₂e / kWh` (or `0.40 g CO₂e / Wh`).

$$\text{Carbon Emitted } (gCO_2e) = \text{Energy (Wh)} \times 0.40$$

---

## 3. Water Evaporative Cooling Footprint (WUE)

Data centers utilize evaporative cooling towers to dissipate heat generated by dense GPU/TPU server racks:
* **Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE):** `0.50 mL per 1,000 tokens` (derived from Shaolei Ren / UC Riverside data center thermal dissipation research).

$$\text{Cooling Water (mL)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Tokens}}{1000} \right) \times 0.50$$

## 4. Ecological Sequestration Equivalents

To translate micro-emissions into relatable, actionable metrics:

1. **Mature Tropical Rainforest Tree:**
   * Absorbs ~`22 kg CO₂ / year` (~`60.27 g CO₂ / day` or ~`0.04185 g CO₂ / minute`).
   $$\text{Tree Absorption Minutes} = \frac{\text{Carbon Emitted (g)}}{0.04185}$$

2. **Mangrove Seedling (Blue Carbon):**
   * Sequester ~`12.3 kg CO₂ / year` (~`33.7 g CO₂ / day`), with 4x higher sediment carbon retention than terrestrial soils.

3. **Coral Reef Buffer:**
   * Coral micro-colonies provide critical wave energy buffering and calcification, impacted by thermal and acidification stress proportional to carbon emissions.

## 5. Dual-Scope Accounting: Turn Delta vs. Cumulative Session

To prevent ambiguity between isolated prompt costs and progressive context growth:

1. **Turn Delta (Incremental Interaction):**
   * Computes the resource footprint strictly from the most recent `USER_INPUT` to the completion of the assistant's reply (including intermediate tool calls).
   * Reflects the marginal cost of the single interaction.

2. **Session Total (Cumulative Thread History):**
   * Computes the cumulative footprint across all turns in the session.
   * Accounts for KV-cache retention and progressive context window inflation.
